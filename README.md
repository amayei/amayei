# Alhassan Mayei

**Digital Health & Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E) Advisor** — data analysis, health-system strengthening, and impact evaluation.

I work at the intersection of digital health, routine health information systems, and applied research, turning programme and survey data into evidence that improves service delivery. I currently support digital health and M&E work with the **Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit (GIZ)** and contribute to research and data-science capacity building at **Njala University**, Sierra Leone.

## Focus areas

- **Digital health** — electronic tools for frontline health workers, stock and commodity management, and digital literacy for health staff.
- **Monitoring & Evaluation** — indicator frameworks, routine monitoring systems, data quality assurance, and results reporting.
- **Applied research & impact evaluation** — household and facility surveys, health-service utilisation and satisfaction, and One Health governance.
- **Data-science capacity building** — training analysts and researchers in reproducible, code-based workflows.

## Current focus: evaluating AI tools before they reach the health system

AI tools are being offered to health systems in low-resource settings with little evidence from the places where they will be used. My current work is on practical, transparent pre-deployment evaluation: a documented construct, an original item bank, a protocol fixed before results, and honest reporting of uncertainty.

| Project | Status | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [**SL-Health-LLM-Eval**](https://github.com/amayei/sl-health-llm-eval) | Pilot, v0.1 | Reproducible benchmark of 48 original public health questions (surveillance, Lassa fever, cholera, malaria, maternal and newborn health, child health, infection control, health information), with a 20-item Krio subset. Zero-shot, option-order shuffling, bootstrap intervals, an answer-key audit and a review gate. Protocol committed before any result. No model results are published yet. |
